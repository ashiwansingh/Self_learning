Table: employees(department, salary)

Har department ki total salary calculate kar, phir total ke basis par category de:
- Total salary 200000 ya zyada → 'High Expense'
- Total salary 100000 ya zyada → 'Medium Expense'
- Baaki → 'Low Expense'
Output mein department, total_salary aur expense_category chahiye.

Hint: SUM(salary) aur GROUP BY department use karna hai. Category individual employee ki salary par nahi, department ki total salary par depend karegi.




Mine Soltion:- 

SELECT 
    CASE 
        WHEN salary>=20000 >= 'High Expense'
        WHEN salary>=10000 >= 'Medium Expense'
        ELSE 'Low Expense'
        END AS 'total_salary'
        SUM(salary) AS 'expense_category'
    END
FROM employees
GROUP BY
    CASE 
        WHEN salary>=20000 >= 'High Expense'
        WHEN salary>=10000 >= 'Medium Expense'
        ELSE 'Low Expense'
    END


Correct Answer: -

SELECT
    department,
    SUM(salary) AS total_salary,
    CASE
        WHEN SUM(salary) >= 200000 THEN 'High Expense'
        WHEN SUM(salary) >= 100000 THEN 'Medium Expense'
        ELSE 'Low Expense'
    END AS expense_category
FROM employees
GROUP BY department;
