Table: employees(name, department, salary)

Har department mein employees ko salary ke highest-to-lowest order mein unique row number dena hai. Numbering har department mein 1 se restart honi chahiye.

Output: name, department, salary, row_num.


Solutions: - 

SELECT name , department , salary , ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY department ORDER BY salary) AS row_num
FROM employees ;
