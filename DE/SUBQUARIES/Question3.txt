Table: employees(name, department, salary)

Task: SQL query likh jo har employee ka name, department, salary aur moving average show kare.

Conditions:
- Har department ki calculation alag honi chahiye.
- Salary highest se lowest order mein ho.
- Average mein current employee aur usse pehle ki 2 rows include honi chahiye.
- Agar previous rows available nahi hain, toh jitni rows available hain unka average nikalo.



Solution : - 

SELECT name , department , salary , AVG(salary) OVER (PARTITION BY department ORDER BY salary ROW BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) AS rn
FROM employees
