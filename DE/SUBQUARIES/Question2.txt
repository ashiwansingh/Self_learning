Table: employees(name, department, salary)

Task: Un sab employees ka name, department aur salary display karo jo aise departments mein kaam karte hain jahan kam-se-kam ek employee ki salary ₹50,000 se zyada hai.

Example: Agar IT department mein kisi ek employee ki salary ₹60,000 hai, toh IT ke saare employees output mein aane chahiye.






SELECT name , department , salary FROM employees WHERE department = (
    SELECT salary
    FROM employees
    WHERE salary >50000
);


Correct One :-



SELECT name, department, salary
FROM employees
WHERE department IN (
    SELECT department
    FROM employees
    WHERE salary > 50000
);
