
Table: employees(name, department, salary)

Task: Har department ke andar employees ko salary ke highest-to-lowest order mein number do. Har department ki numbering 1 se restart honi chahiye.

Hint: PARTITION BY use karna hai.




Solution: -  



SELECT name, department, salary,
       ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
           PARTITION BY department
           ORDER BY salary DESC
       ) AS rn
FROM employees;
