Question: - 
Table: employees(name, department, salary)

SQL query likho jo har department ke employees ko salary ke highest-to-lowest order mein rank kare. Same salary walon ko same rank mile aur ranks mein koi gap na ho.

Output: name, department, salary, salary_rank.






Solutions :- 

SELECT name , department , salary , DENSE_RANK(salary) OVER (PARTITION BY department ORDER BY salary DESC) AS salary_rank
