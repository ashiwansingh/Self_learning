Table: employees(name, department, salary)

Write an SQL query to display each employee's name, department, salary, and running total salary within their department.

Conditions:
- Har department ki calculation alag ho.
- Employees ko salary ke lowest-to-highest order mein arrange karo.
- Running total current employee tak ki salaries ka sum ho.
Output columns: name, department, salary, running_total.





Solutions :-




SELECT name , department , salary 
SUM(salary) OVER(PARTITION department ORDER BY salary DESC ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) AS running_total
FROM employees
